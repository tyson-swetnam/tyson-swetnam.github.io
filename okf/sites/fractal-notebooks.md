# fractal-notebooks

Fractal Notebooks: self-affine fractals and metabolic scaling theory. Part I teaching
chapters (history, mathematics, dimensionality, glossary), four research manuscripts
(metabolic scaling, spectral geometry, biological geometry, forest-canopy hypotheses),
pre-rendered Jupyter notebooks, React and Streamlit apps, and two Open Knowledge Format
(OKF v0.2) bundles: 32 concepts generated from the RAG concept seed and 12 hand-written
proofs with `[verified]`/`[asserted]` step tags.

This is a project sub-site of [tyson-swetnam.github.io](/) served at
<https://tyson-swetnam.github.io/fractal-notebooks/>. It is published from the GitHub repository
